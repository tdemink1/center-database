### The big picture
U.S. stocks ended the week lower as oil surged and long-term yields stayed near 5%. The S&P 500 slipped 0.5% to about 7,624, the Dow Jones fell 1.2% to about 52,215, the Nasdaq Composite lost 0.2% and the Russell 2000 eased 0.3%. The VIX rose about 2% to 19.4. The theme was an energy-and-rates squeeze: the Dow, heavy in cyclicals and financials, lagged, while software and memory-chip names held up.

### What's driving it
- **Oil on Iran fears:** WTI crude rose about 4% to roughly $103 a barrel on reports that renewed U.S. and Israeli strikes on Iran are being planned, which fed inflation worries.
- **Yields near 5%:** The 10-year Treasury yield held around 4.99% (+3 bp), with the 30-year near 5.3%, keeping pressure on rate-sensitive and cyclical stocks.
- **Cyclicals and financials weak:** Caterpillar, JPMorgan, Mastercard and Berkshire fell, and American Express dropped after announcing job cuts, according to market reports.
- **Dollar and gold:** Gold fell about 1.2% to near $4,010 as the dollar index edged up to about 101.8.

### Rates & macro
S&P Global's flash U.S. PMIs, as reported by market outlets, showed manufacturing near 56.0 (forecast about 55.4–55.5) and services near 57.9, slightly softer than September. Strong activity readings with high energy prices reinforce the case for tighter policy. The Fed meets Oct 27–28, and a hold at 3.75%–4.00% is the strong consensus, with markets focused on a possible December hike.

### Calendar & forecasts
- **Tue Oct 27:** September durable goods, Conference Board consumer confidence, new home sales (no consensus found).
- **Wed Oct 28:** FOMC decision at 2:00 pm ET; the Bank of Canada (markets price roughly an 80% chance of a hike from 2.25%) and RBNZ also decide. Microsoft and Alphabet report after the close.
- **Thu Oct 29:** Advance Q3 GDP (consensus about 3.0%, previous 2.2%), September PCE (core consensus +0.3% m/m, 3.1% y/y) and the ECB decision, where a hold is expected. Amazon reports.
- **Fri Oct 30:** Bank of Japan, with a hold at 1.25% expected.

### Global
- **Europe:** Five of six major indexes fell; Germany's DAX lost 0.75% while Italy's FTSE MIB gained 0.36%.
- **Asia-Pacific:** Six of seven fell; Korea's KOSPI dropped 3.2% and Australia's ASX 200 was flat.
- **Commodities & FX:** Brent was near $112, natural gas rose over 9%, and copper gained about 2%. The dollar firmed against the euro and pound while the yen strengthened, with USD/JPY near 156.9.
- **Crypto:** Bitcoin was roughly flat near $81,300; Ethereum near $2,390.

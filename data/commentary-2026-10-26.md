### The big picture
U.S. stocks started a big week with solid gains. The S&P 500 rose 0.70%, the Dow 0.28%, the Nasdaq Composite 0.82% and the Russell 2000 0.38%. The VIX eased 5.5% to 18.39. The theme was a tech-led rebound ahead of this week's Fed decision and a heavy run of megacap earnings, with oil lower and the 10-year yield steady just under 5%.

### What's driving it
- **AI and tech bid:** Coverage tied the rally to renewed AI enthusiasm around Anthropic's IPO announcement. Semiconductor ETF SMH gained 2.7%, with MU up 3.0%, AMD 2.8%, AVGO 2.3% and NVDA 2.4%.
- **Tesla:** TSLA jumped 5.2% after a Barclays upgrade to Overweight with a $450 target, which cited a possible SpaceX combination.
- **Energy relief:** WTI crude fell 1.8% to about $101, though it remains elevated.
- **Positioning:** Investors were looking to rebound from last week's losses ahead of the FOMC meeting.

### Rates & macro
The 10-year Treasury yield held near 4.99%, flat on the day, and the 5-year was about 4.82%. No major U.S. data came out today. Markets broadly expect the Fed to hold at 3.75%–4.00% on Wednesday, with roughly a 10% chance priced for a hike, and analysts expect a hawkish tone from Chair Warsh.

### Calendar & forecasts
- **Tuesday Oct 27:** Conference Board consumer confidence (forecast 81.2, previous 81.9). RBNZ decision, where most expect a hike to 3.00%.
- **Wednesday Oct 28:** FOMC decision (hold expected) and the Bank of Canada (hike to 2.50% priced at about 80%).
- **Thursday Oct 29:** Advance Q3 GDP (forecast about 3.0%, previous 2.2%), September PCE (core forecast 3.1% y/y) and the ECB (hold expected).
- **Friday Oct 30:** Bank of Japan (hold expected) and the Q3 Employment Cost Index.

### Global
Europe closed higher across the board: FTSE MIB +1.29%, DAX +0.91%, Euro Stoxx 50 +0.79% and FTSE 100 +0.72%. In Asia, Hang Seng gained 1.21% and Shanghai 1.08%, while Taiwan's TAIEX fell 1.81% and the Nikkei 1.14%. Gold rose 0.48%. The dollar index firmed to about 102.0 and EUR/USD slipped to 1.118. Bitcoin was near $79,800 (-0.3%).

### Watchlist takeaways
Of 33 names, 21 finished higher. Leaders were TSLA (+5.2%), GEV (+4.9%) and MU (+3.0%). Laggards were MSTR (-4.2%), COIN (-2.9%) and META (-1.9%), with IBIT down 1.6% as crypto softened. Tesla's third-quarter free cash flow fell 62% to $1.53 billion even as the upgrade lifted the shares. Megacap tech earnings later this week are the next catalyst for many holdings.

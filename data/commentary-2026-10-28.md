### The big picture
U.S. stocks edged higher in a quiet session as the Federal Reserve held rates steady. The S&P 500 gained 0.28% to 7,743.98, the Dow rose 0.19% to 52,475.10, the Nasdaq Composite added 0.27% and the Russell 2000 advanced 0.41%. The VIX slipped 3.5% to 16.91. All three major indexes remain slightly below last Wednesday's levels (S&P 500 -0.17% on the week), so the theme was relief that the Fed did not tighten, not a breakout.

### What's driving it
- **Fed on hold, with a hawkish edge:** The FOMC left the target range at 3.75%–4.00%, but press coverage reports two dissents in favor of a hike (Governor Cook and Cleveland Fed President Hammack) and describes economic activity as "strong."
- **Warsh keeps December open:** Chair Warsh said the Fed is entering an "information-rich" period and he is not offering forward guidance, per press coverage; investors still lean toward a December hike.
- **AI and chips:** Micron (+5.86%), CoreWeave (+7.32%) and AMD (+3.24%) led the watchlist, and MarketWatch reported Oracle shares jumping on new financial targets that eased worries about AI economics.
- **Software wobble:** ServiceNow fell after an earnings miss, even with a raised AI outlook, per MarketWatch.
- **Energy:** WTI crude rose 1.76% to $101.66, while Brent futures fell 3.24%.

### Rates & macro
The 10-year Treasury yield rose about 5 bp to 5.04% and the 30-year to 5.36%; the 5-year yield was 4.87%. The Fed held at 3.75%–4.00% as forecast. Tomorrow's data are the main event risk: advance Q3 GDP and September PCE inflation.

### Calendar & forecasts
- **Thursday, Oct 29:** Advance Q3 GDP (forecasts of about 3.0%–3.2% annualized; sources differ), September core PCE (+0.3% m/m forecast; one preview has headline PCE at 3.6% y/y and core at 3.1% y/y), and the ECB decision (hold expected).
- **Friday, Oct 30:** Bank of Japan decision (hold at 1.25% expected, with a December hike priced at about 80% in one preview), plus the Q3 Employment Cost Index.
- **Next week:** ISM Manufacturing on Nov 2, the Bank of England on Nov 5 (a hike to 4.00% is widely expected) and the October jobs report on Nov 6.
- **Later:** Markets lean toward a Fed hike on Dec 9.

### Global
Europe was mixed: the DAX gained 0.44%, FTSE MIB 0.40%, FTSE 100 0.07%, and Switzerland's SMI slipped 0.11%. In Asia, the Nikkei rose 2.84%, Taiwan 2.16% and Hong Kong's Hang Seng fell 0.50%. The KOSPI data point (+9.93%) looks anomalous and could not be verified, so treat it with caution. Gold rose 0.18% to $4,081.80, the dollar index slipped 0.19% to 102.13, and bitcoin gained 0.38% to about $81,110.

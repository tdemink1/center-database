**Weekly Wrap: week of Oct 26–Oct 30, 2026**

### The big picture
U.S. stocks ended the week with a sharp Friday selloff led by technology. The S&P 500 fell 1.82% on the day to 7,580 (-0.57% for the week), the Dow lost 1.09% to 51,770 (-0.85%), the Nasdaq Composite dropped 2.86% (-2.00%), and the Russell 2000 slid 1.66% but still gained 1.29% on the week. The VIX jumped 12.9% to 19.42. The theme was growing doubt over AI spending after disappointing big-tech and chip earnings.

### What's driving it
- **AI-spending worries:** Amazon missed on revenue and earnings, and management signaled meaningfully higher AI spending in 2027, with a large free cash outflow. Reports also noted Amazon drawing on a credit line to fund AI capex.
- **Intel's warning:** Intel flagged weak PC demand and trouble producing enough AI server chips, which hit chip stocks. AMD fell 10.9% and the semiconductor ETF SMH lost 7.1%.
- **Valuation reset:** Commentary described tech as priced for perfection, and the Nasdaq-100 fell 3.46% on the day.
- **Crypto stress:** Reports of a roughly $6 billion hack at Binance weighed on crypto-linked names. COIN fell 11.6% and MSTR 7.8%.

### Rates & macro
The 10-year Treasury yield eased about 6 bp to 4.93%, and the 30-year was near 5.31%. The Fed held rates at 3.75%–4.00% on Wednesday, with two dissents in favor of a hike; Dallas Fed President Logan said she sees at least 50 bp of additional hikes. This week's calendar listed Q3 advance GDP (forecast 3.2%), core PCE (forecast +0.3% m/m) and the Employment Cost Index (forecast 0.8%); we could not verify the actual readings, so check the BEA releases. One report said wage growth hit a five-year low and lagged inflation for a second straight quarter.

### Week ahead

- **Thu Nov 5:** Bank of England decision; a hike to 4.00% from 3.75% is widely expected.
- **Fri Nov 6:** U.S. October jobs report (previous payrolls +29K, unemployment 4.2%; consensus not verified).

### Global
Europe was mixed: Euro Stoxx 50 -1.22% on the day but +1.04% on the week, DAX -0.85% (+2.53%), FTSE 100 +0.07%, SMI +0.36%. In Asia, KOSPI fell 4.69% and Taiwan's index 3.88%, while the Sensex rose 1.66% and the Hang Seng 0.86%; Nikkei -1.04%. WTI crude rose 1.99% to about $104, while gold fell 3.55% to roughly $3,888 and silver 6.01%. The dollar index was little changed near 101.9. Bitcoin slipped 1.3% to about $76,750 (-5.3% on the week).

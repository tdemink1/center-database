### The big picture
U.S. stocks rallied broadly on Wednesday after Democrats won control of Congress and oil prices plunged. The Dow Jones jumped 3.03% to 53,356, its best day in roughly 18 months, while the S&P 500 gained 1.58% to 7,688.62, the Nasdaq Composite rose 1.44% and the Russell 2000 added 1.58%. The VIX fell 20.3% to 16.61. Leadership was uneven: the Nasdaq-100 gained only 0.91% and remains about 4.6% lower on the week.

### What's driving it
- **Election relief:** Markets welcomed the clearer political outcome, with investors hoping a divided government pushes toward a faster end to the Iran war.
- **Oil collapse:** WTI fell 7.94% to $88.46 and Brent fell 8.26% to $95.04, easing inflation worries; WMB and LNG dropped with energy.
- **AI infrastructure under pressure:** Data-center and chip-related names sold off as investors rotated, with SMH down 2.75% and MU down 7.0%.
- **Rotation to defensives and large caps:** Apple, Microsoft and Amazon rose strongly, while financials and consumer names also advanced.

### Rates & macro
The 10-year Treasury yield slipped about 6 bp to 4.89%, the 5-year to 4.69% and the 30-year to 5.28%. The October ISM Services PMI came in at 54.5, a little below the 54.8 forecast and down from 54.9, with the prices-paid gauge easing to 73 per press reports. The Fed held rates on October 28 with two dissents in favor of a hike; as of Monday, futures priced a high chance of a December hike, which we have not re-verified after today's move in yields.

### Calendar & forecasts
- **Thursday:** The Bank of England decision at 7:00 a.m. ET, with a hike to 4.00% from 3.75% expected and a 7-0-2 vote forecast. U.S. jobless claims are forecast at 197K.
- **Friday:** The U.S. October jobs report: payrolls forecast at +65K (prior +29K), unemployment 4.2%, average hourly earnings +0.3% m/m. Preliminary Michigan sentiment is forecast at 48.1 (prior 46.3).
- **Next month:** FOMC on December 9; ECB and BoE decisions on December 17.

### Global
European markets all closed higher: DAX +2.22%, CAC 40 +1.96%, FTSE 100 +1.79%. Asia was mixed: Hang Seng +2.25% and Sensex +2.13%, but TAIEX fell 4.39%, KOSPI 2.99% and Nikkei 1.74%. Gold rose 3.43% to about $4,075 and silver 5.61%. The dollar index slipped to 101.5 and USD/JPY was near 158.4. Bitcoin gained 3.47% to about $80,029 and Ether 6.36%.

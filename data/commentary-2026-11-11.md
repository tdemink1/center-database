### The big picture
U.S. stocks rallied Wednesday as AI-related names rebounded. The S&P 500 rose 1.31% to 7,619.38, the Dow gained 0.49% to 52,486.49, the Nasdaq Composite climbed 1.60% to 25,466.19 and the Nasdaq 100 added 2.28%. The Russell 2000 rose 0.88%. The VIX fell 10.1% to 17.49. The S&P 500 is still about 0.9% below last Friday's close.

### What's driving it
- **CoreWeave earnings revived the AI trade.** CRWV jumped 24.7% after reporting stronger revenue and raising its full-year outlook, easing worries about AI spending.
- **Chips and memory led.** MU rose 11.9%, AMD 7.3%, AVGO 4.0% and the SMH ETF 4.8%. NVDA gained 2.9% after steep losses earlier in the week, though it has still fallen 13.2% over five sessions.
- **Mega-cap software and platforms lagged.** MSFT fell 2.3%, META 2.0% and AMZN 1.7%.
- **Inflation worries remain in the background** after Tuesday's CPI (below).

### Rates & macro
The 10-year Treasury yield was about 5.00% (+2 bp) and the 5-year 4.83%. Tuesday's October CPI was hotter than expected: headline +0.4% m/m against a 0.3% forecast, core +0.3% against 0.2%. There is no year-over-year figure for October because of data gaps from last year's shutdown. The Fed held at 3.75%–4.00% on Oct 28, with two dissents favoring a hike; the next meeting is Dec 8–9. Updated market pricing for a hike was not verified.

### Calendar & forecasts
- **Thursday Nov 12:** U.S. initial jobless claims (forecast 194K, previous 189K); UK GDP; ECB President Lagarde speaks.
- **Friday Nov 13:** U.S. PPI m/m (forecast 0.4%, previous 1.3%) and core PPI (forecast 0.3%, previous 0.7%).
- **Central banks:** FOMC Dec 8–9; ECB and Bank of England Dec 17; Bank of Japan Dec 18.

### Global
Europe closed higher across the board: DAX +1.20%, Euro Stoxx 50 +1.04%, FTSE 100 +0.78%. In Asia, KOSPI surged 7.10% and the Nikkei gained 4.21%, while the Hang Seng slipped 0.54%. WTI crude rose 0.4% to $86.40 and Brent 1.1% to $92.53. Gold fell 0.95% to about $3,992. The dollar index gained 0.7% to 102.93, with EUR/USD down 1.2% to 1.109. Bitcoin rose 1.3% to about $74,700.

### Watchlist takeaways
26 of 33 holdings rose. Leaders were CRWV (+24.7%), MU (+11.9%) and AMD (+7.3%); laggards were SPCX (-2.3%), MSFT (-2.3%) and META (-2.0%). NVDA drew a second analyst downgrade this week ahead of its report, yet rebounded with the group. Over the week, MSTR (-14.2%), COIN (-10.1%) and NVDA (-13.2%) remain the weakest names.

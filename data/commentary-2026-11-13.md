**Weekly Wrap: week of Nov 9–Nov 13**

### The big picture
U.S. stocks ended Friday mostly lower but still posted their first winning week since early October. The S&P 500 slipped 0.40% to 7,561.67 (+2.5% on the week), the Dow rose 0.10% to 52,347.55 (+2.1%), the Nasdaq Composite fell 0.91% (+2.3%) and the Russell 2000 lost 0.31% (+1.3%). The VIX rose to 19.30 but fell about 25% over the week. The theme: a post-midterm rally and AI-infrastructure demand lifted indexes early, while a chip selloff and a hot consumer-price report kept gains uneven.

### What's driving it
- **Chip weakness:** Nvidia's CEO transition (Jensen Huang resigned Nov 12; CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO) kept pressure on semiconductors. NVDA fell 2.0% Friday and 12.7% on the week; SMH lost 3.0% on the day.
- **AI trade rethink:** Reports said chip peers failed to benefit from Nvidia's slide. AMD fell 8.75% and AVGO 4.76% Friday, though both are up about 18-19% for the week.
- **Inflation:** Wednesday's hot October CPI stayed in focus; coverage described today's PPI as cooler than expected, but we could not verify the actual figures.
- **Crypto and risk appetite:** Bitcoin fell 4.3% to about $68,270 (-9.9% on the week).

### Rates & macro
The 10-year Treasury yield stood at 5.15% (up 5 bp Friday, about 6% higher on the week); the 30-year was 5.48%. October CPI ran hotter than forecast earlier in the week (headline +0.4% vs 0.3% expected, core +0.3% vs 0.2%). Friday's PPI consensus was +0.4% (core +0.3%) versus prior +1.3% (core +0.7%). The Fed held at 3.75%-4.00% on Oct 28 with two dissents favoring a hike; futures were reported to price a high chance of a December hike (about 81% as of Nov 8, not refreshed).

### Week ahead
- **Fed:** FOMC minutes and retail sales are expected in the week of Nov 16 (exact dates and consensus not verified); the next FOMC decision is Dec 9.
- **Earnings:** Nvidia is reported to report Nov 17 alongside Home Depot and Disney; Target follows Nov 18 and Walmart Nov 19. Confirm Nvidia's date given the leadership change.
- **Central banks:** ECB and Bank of England decide Dec 17; Bank of Japan Dec 18.

### Global
Europe was mixed: FTSE 100 +0.36%, DAX -0.08%, CAC 40 +0.09%, with the major indexes up roughly 2-4% on the week. Asia was weak: Hang Seng -2.79%, Shanghai -1.26%, KOSPI -1.58%, Nikkei -0.17%. WTI crude rose 0.2% to $88.92 and Brent was $96.16; gold slipped 0.14% to $3,880 (-4.8% on the week). The dollar index rose 0.21% to 103.38, with EUR/USD down 0.63% and USD/JPY near 159.7. Ethereum fell 4.4%.

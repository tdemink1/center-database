### The big picture
U.S. stocks sold off sharply on Monday, led by technology. The S&P 500 fell 1.92% to 7,416.59, the Dow lost 1.41% to 51,608.17, the Nasdaq Composite dropped 2.49% and the Russell 2000 slid 1.70%. The VIX jumped 18.7% to 22.91. The theme was a deepening chip and AI selloff ahead of Nvidia's earnings on Tuesday after the close.

### What's driving it
- **Chip and AI selloff:** Micron fell 7.6%, AMD 5.6%, ASML 5.9% and Nvidia 4.1%, which is down roughly 25% over the past month; the SMH chip ETF lost 5.1%. News reports pointed to investor nerves over Nvidia's results and whether it will scale back financing of the AI ecosystem.
- **Nvidia backdrop:** Reports cite leadership uncertainty after CEO Jensen Huang's resignation and a probe now focused on chip smuggling into China.
- **Crypto-linked names:** MicroStrategy (-7.7%) and Coinbase (-6.1%) fell sharply, with IBIT down 5.5%, though Bitcoin itself was little changed near $65,185.
- **Cyclicals:** Caterpillar fell 5.0% after its worst week in about 18 months.

### Rates & macro
The 10-year Treasury yield was 5.12% (down 3 bp), the 5-year 4.94% and the 30-year 5.47%. Fed Chair Warsh spoke at noon ET; I could not verify what he said. October CPI ran hotter than expected last week, and the Fed's next decision is Dec. 9. I found no current FedWatch reading, so December rate expectations are unverified here.

### Calendar & forecasts
- **Tuesday:** U.S. retail sales, with a consensus of +0.2% m/m (previous +0.8%) and core +0.4% (previous +0.9%); Nvidia reports after the bell.
- **Wednesday:** FOMC minutes from the Oct. 28 meeting (2:00 pm ET) and U.K. CPI (forecast 3.6% y/y vs 3.5%).
- **Thursday:** Jobless claims (forecast 199K) and the Philly Fed index (forecast 19.2).
- **Friday:** Final University of Michigan sentiment (forecast 52.1).

### Global
Europe was mostly lower: Euro Stoxx 50 -0.6%, DAX -0.6%, FTSE 100 -0.2%, with Switzerland's SMI up 0.6%. In Asia, Taiwan's TAIEX fell 4.8% and South Korea's KOSPI 4.5%, both hit by the chip selloff, while the Nikkei lost 1.9% and Hang Seng 1.1%. WTI crude dropped 3.6% to $85.36, gold fell 0.8% to $3,855 and silver 4.3%. The dollar index edged up 0.2%; Bitcoin was near $65,185.

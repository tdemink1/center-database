### The big picture
U.S. stocks ended mixed on Wednesday as the Fed minutes and a pullback in energy and metals kept sentiment cautious. The **S&P 500** slipped 0.23% to 7,323.63 and the **Dow** fell 1.11% to 50,198.17, while the **Nasdaq Composite** gained 0.42% and the **Russell 2000** added 0.51%. The **VIX** eased 3.5% to 24.56 but stayed elevated. The S&P 500 is down about 3.9% on the week and 4.8% on the month.

### What's driving it
- **Fed minutes:** Minutes of the October meeting showed growing unease among officials about the inflation outlook even as rates were held, reinforcing rate-hike worries.
- **Nvidia aftermath:** Nvidia's results and outlook drew a muted reaction, and the stock fell 2.9% (about 13% lower on the week), weighing on the AI complex.
- **Rotation:** Software and cybersecurity outperformed, led by CrowdStrike, while the Dow lagged.
- **Commodity slide:** Crude, gold, silver and copper all fell sharply, a risk-off signal for cyclical names.

### Rates & macro
The 10-year Treasury yield eased about 4 bp to 5.07%, the 5-year to 4.88% and the 30-year to 5.42%. The Fed minutes (2:00 pm ET) were the day's main event. The October CPI (Nov 10) ran hotter than forecast, and the Fed held at 3.75%–4.00% on Oct 28 with two dissents favoring a hike. Next FOMC decision: Dec 9. I could not verify a current CME FedWatch reading.

### Global

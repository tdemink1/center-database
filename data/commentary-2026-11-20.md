### The big picture
U.S. stocks suffered a violent Friday selloff. The S&P 500 fell 3.70% to 6,910, the Nasdaq Composite dropped 5.28% to 22,618 and the Nasdaq-100 lost 5.61%. The Dow gave up 1.93% and the Russell 2000 fell 2.76%. The VIX jumped 26% to 35.89. The theme was a renewed AI and tech rout, with only 1 of 33 watchlist names finishing higher.

### What's driving it
- **AI valuation worries:** Press reports tied the slide to doubts that heavy AI spending can produce sustainable profits, even as Anthropic's record IPO traded near its offer price after a sharp drop.
- **Rate-hike expectations:** Hot October inflation and Fed minutes showing unease have markets pricing a December hike, which pressures long-duration growth stocks.
- **Semiconductors led losses:** NVDA, MU, AMD and AVGO fell 12-14% each, and SMH dropped 10.6%.
- **Haven assets also sold:** Gold fell 4.56% and silver 8.13%, a sign of broad de-risking and liquidation. Bitcoin lost 5.35%.

### Rates & macro
The 10-year Treasury yield eased about 14 bp to 4.91%, with the 5-year at 4.70% and the 30-year at 5.30%. Final University of Michigan sentiment came in at 51.7 (consensus 51.7, prior 51.0). Futures pricing reported earlier this week put a 25 bp Fed hike on Dec 9-10 at roughly 90-95%; I could not find a same-day reading.

### Calendar & forecasts
Thanksgiving falls on Thursday Nov 26, so U.S. markets are closed that day and the week is short. The Q3 GDP second estimate and October PCE are reportedly due around Nov 25 (date unconfirmed, no consensus found). Next central-bank decisions: FOMC Dec 9-10, ECB and Bank of England Dec 17 (a Reuters poll expected an ECB hike to 2.75%), Bank of Japan Dec 18.

### Global
Europe fell across the board: Euro Stoxx 50 -2.81%, DAX -2.60%, FTSE MIB -2.48%, CAC 40 -1.89%, FTSE 100 and SMI about -1.0%. Asia was hit hard: Taiwan's TAIEX -10.35%, Nikkei -4.38%, KOSPI -3.90%, Hang Seng -3.79%. India's Sensex rose 2.30%. WTI crude rose 0.59% to $83.70 and Brent fell 2.19%. The dollar index was flat at 103.89. Ethereum fell 8.39%.

### The big picture
U.S. stocks staged a sharp rebound on Monday after last week's selloff. The S&P 500 gained 4.73% to 7,237.17, the Dow rose 3.01% to 49,595.31, the Nasdaq Composite jumped 6.51% to 24,089.56 and the Russell 2000 added 3.40%. The VIX fell 25.8% to 26.62 but remains elevated. Press reports describe it as the best session since April 2025, led by a surge in Nvidia.

### What's driving it
- **Nvidia reversal:** NVDA rose 31.5% to about $165 after reports that its board reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO, reviving the AI trade.
- **AI and semiconductors:** CRWV (+22.3%), MU (+19.0%) and SMH (+15.0%) followed; 30 of 33 watchlist names finished higher.
- **Oversold bounce:** the move followed a roughly 10% Nasdaq drop last week, and weekly index changes are still negative (S&P 500 -2.4%, Nasdaq -1.7%).
- **Laggards:** CRWD fell 8.9%, BFGIX 3.7% and CB 0.8%.

### Rates & macro
The 10-year Treasury yield was 4.92% (+1 bp) and the 30-year 5.29%. Flash PMIs came in for the U.S. (manufacturing and services both expected to ease from prior readings of 56.0 and 57.9; see the calendar feed for actuals). Markets continue to price a likely 25 bp Fed hike at the Dec 9 meeting after a hot October CPI; no fresh FedWatch reading was verified today.

### Calendar & forecasts
- **Tuesday, Nov 24:** Conference Board consumer confidence, forecast 92.4 (previous 91.7).
- **Wednesday, Nov 25:** preliminary Q3 GDP, forecast 3.5% (previous 3.4%), and core PCE, forecast 0.3% m/m (previous 0.3%), both at 8:30 ET.
- **Thursday, Nov 26:** Thanksgiving; U.S. markets closed.
- **Further out:** FOMC Dec 9, ECB and BoE Dec 17, BoJ Dec 18.

### Global
Europe closed higher across the board: Euro Stoxx 50 +2.23%, DAX +2.15%, FTSE 100 +1.30%. Asia-Pacific was weaker: KOSPI -8.36%, Nikkei -5.06%, Taiwan -4.20%, Hang Seng -2.00%. WTI crude fell 4.4% to $80.28, gold rose 3.4% to $3,635.80, and the dollar index gained 0.5% to 104.33. Bitcoin was little changed near $61,350.

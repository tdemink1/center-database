### The big picture
U.S. stocks ended mixed on Wednesday after recovering most of their steep early losses as an AI-stock pullback eased. The S&P 500 fell 0.25% to 7,182.78 and the Nasdaq Composite lost 0.62%, while the Dow rose 0.33% to 50,182.95 and the Russell 2000 gained 0.71%. The VIX rose 7.3% to 28.29, still elevated. The theme was rotation out of AI-linked large caps and into broader, smaller companies.

### What's driving it
- **AI-stock weakness:** Nvidia fell 3.9% and Micron 4.5%; press coverage noted Nvidia heading toward its first "death cross" since March 2025.
- **Fed hike worries:** Headlines on Wednesday said the Fed sees inflation as stubborn ahead of its December vote on a possible rate increase.
- **Falling yields:** The 10-year Treasury yield eased about 6 bp, helping cyclical and small-cap shares.
- **Housing:** New-home sales reportedly hit a 2026 high as builders offered incentives.

### Rates & macro
The 10-year yield closed near 4.85% and the 30-year near 5.24%. Preliminary Q3 GDP and core PCE were scheduled for 8:30 ET (consensus: GDP 3.5% q/q vs. 3.4% previously; core PCE 0.3% m/m, unchanged); the actual readings were not available in our data feed, so they are not reported here. The Fed funds range is 3.75%–4.00%; the latest futures pricing we could verify (mid-November) pointed to a better than 90% chance of a 25 bp hike on Dec 9.

### Calendar & forecasts
U.S. markets are closed Thursday for Thanksgiving, with an early bond close expected Friday. Japan's Tokyo Core CPI is due Thursday evening (forecast 3.0% y/y vs. 2.6%). The November jobs report is expected around Dec 4 (consensus not yet verified). The FOMC meets Dec 9; the ECB and Bank of England decide Dec 17 and the Bank of Japan Dec 18, with a Reuters poll showing economists expecting an ECB hike to 2.75%.

### Global
European stocks fell across the board: Euro Stoxx 50 -1.82%, DAX -1.26%, FTSE 100 -0.38%. In Asia, Taiwan's TAIEX jumped 3.76% and the Nikkei rose 1.02%, while Hang Seng fell 1.87% and the Shanghai Composite 1.77%. WTI crude rose 2.6% to about $84.16, natural gas jumped 10.8%, gold gained 1.0% to roughly $3,598 and copper 2.1%. The dollar index slipped 0.1%; EUR/USD rose 0.64%. Bitcoin was up 0.8% near $60,246.

**Weekly Wrap: week of Nov 23–Nov 27**

### The big picture
U.S. stocks rallied into the holiday-shortened Friday close. The S&P 500 gained 1.60% on the day to 7,297 (+5.6% for the week), the Dow rose 1.18% to 50,775 (+5.5%), the Nasdaq Composite jumped 2.31% (+6.7%) and the Russell 2000 added 1.71% (+6.5%). The VIX fell 11% to 23.81, down about 34% on the week. Thursday was the Thanksgiving closure.

### What's driving it
- **Tech and semiconductors led:** the Nasdaq-100 rose 2.61% on the day; SMH gained 4.17% and is up 10% on the week.
- **Volatility compression:** the sharp drop in the VIX points to a relief rally after a rough stretch, though one MarketWatch weekend piece flagged warnings behind the rally.
- **Metals surge:** gold rose 3.75% to about $3,669 and silver 9.1%, with copper up 5.0%.
- **Nvidia rebound:** MarketWatch noted Nvidia soared this week but remains down about 28% for November.

### Rates & macro
The 10-year Treasury yield rose about 9 bp to 4.94% (30-year 5.33%). The Fed held at 3.75%–4.00% on Oct 28 with two dissents favoring a hike; the last pricing we could verify (Nov 17) showed a December hike as very likely. We could not verify a fresher FedWatch reading, so treat that as dated.

### Week ahead
- **Dec 4:** U.S. November jobs report (consensus not yet verified).
- **Dec 9:** FOMC decision.
- **Dec 17:** ECB and Bank of England decisions; a Reuters poll had economists expecting an ECB hike to 2.75%, and the BoE is at 4.00%.
- **Dec 18:** Bank of Japan.

### Global
Europe closed higher across the board (Euro Stoxx 50 +1.24%, FTSE 100 +0.84%, DAX +0.90%). Asia was strong: KOSPI +4.64%, Shanghai +3.74%, Hang Seng +3.32%, Nikkei +2.98%; India's Sensex fell 0.71%. WTI crude slipped 0.72% to $83.08 and Brent fell 3.98%. The dollar index rose 0.36% to 104.05. Bitcoin fell 1.24% to about $59,746 and Ethereum 1.42%.

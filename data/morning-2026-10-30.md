### Overnight
- **Asia-Pacific was mixed.** Hong Kong's Hang Seng led (+1.46%), Japan's Nikkei 225 slipped about 1.0%, and South Korea's KOSPI fell 4.69%. The Bank of Japan held its press conference early Friday.
- **Europe was mostly lower.** The Euro Stoxx 50 fell about 1.05%; Switzerland's SMI was a rare gainer (+0.38%).
- **Big Tech earnings dented the AI trade.** Headlines point to weak results from Amazon and a disappointing outlook from Intel, with shrinking cash flows cited as a common thread.
- **Crypto stocks slid** after a reported Binance security breach with an estimated $6.3 billion impact.

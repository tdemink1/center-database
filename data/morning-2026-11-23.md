### Overnight
- **Asia-Pacific fell broadly** while Europe rallied. The KOSPI dropped about 8.4%, the Nikkei 225 about 5.1% and Taiwan's TAIEX about 4.2%. Hang Seng lost 2.0% and the ASX 200 slipped 0.3%.
- **Europe was higher across the board** at midday: Euro Stoxx 50 +2.2%, DAX +2.2%, CAC 40 +1.5%, FTSE 100 +1.3%.
- **Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as president and CEO**, sending the stock up by the most in a decade and lifting semiconductor and AI-linked names.
- **CrowdStrike agreed to buy Zscaler** in a roughly $43.9 billion deal. CrowdStrike shares fell about 12%.
- **Health insurers** UnitedHealth, Humana and CVS slid on scrutiny of Medicare Advantage ratings.

### Early trading
U.S. stocks opened sharply higher. Around 11 a.m. ET the **S&P 500 was near 7,256 (+5.0%)**, the **Nasdaq Composite +6.8%** and the **Dow +3.1%**. The VIX eased to about 27.5 (-23%). The 10-year Treasury yield was about 4.94% (+3 bp), WTI crude about $80 (-4.6%), gold about $3,674 (+4.5%) and bitcoin about $62,600 (+2.4%).

### Data & events
- **Flash PMIs:** U.S. manufacturing and services readings were released at 9:45 ET (forecasts 54.5 and 55.6; prior 56.0 and 57.9). S&P surveys reportedly point to roughly 3% annualized U.S. growth in Q4.
- **Europe and UK flash PMIs** were also out this morning.

- **Ahead:** AI-related earnings this Thanksgiving week.
